About the job



Role summary

This is a specialist HVAC controls contractor looking to add a junior to mid-level Controls Service Technician to the growing team nationwide. You would be working fully remote, with limited travel nationwide. This is a growth hire within a growing and established controls company that values training, support, and long‑term development. The role is primarily service‑focused, with exposure to preventative maintenance and small projects, working across Niagara‑based building automation systems in commercial buildings and critical environments.


About the organisation

The business is an established HVAC controls contractor operating nationwide. The team is deliberately kept selective, collaborative, and is seeking to grow, with an emphasis on doing work properly, supporting technicians, and building long‑term client relationships. Engineers are given exposure to real systems, practical troubleshooting, and hands‑on learning rather than being siloed into repetitive tasks.


Key responsibilities

Carry out service, fault finding, and troubleshooting on HVAC controls and BAS systems
Perform preventative maintenance and system health checks
Support small works and minor upgrades alongside senior engineers
Diagnose control issues related to sensors, controllers, networks, and field devices
Work with Niagara‑based systems in critical environments
Produce clear service reports and communicate findings with customers and the internal team
Build familiarity with clients over time
Participate in a limited on‑call rota (low frequency)

Required experience and skills

2+ years of hands‑on experience in HVAC controls or building automation
Background in service, maintenance, or support of BAS / BMS systems
Practical understanding of HVAC plant and control sequences
Exposure to Niagara frameworks (training level or field experience acceptable)
Comfortable working independently on site with support when needed
Full eligibility to work in the United States, and valid passport and/or REAL ID.

Desirable experience

Experience with small controls projects or minor modifications
Familiarity with common field devices and control panels
Basic networking or protocol awareness within building systems
Interest in developing towards a more senior service or project role

Systems and technologies

Niagara‑based BMS platforms
Typical commercial HVAC control environments
Service, maintenance, and small project works

Location and working pattern

Fully remote service role (limited travel)
Limited on‑call requirement
Day‑to‑day work centred around service and planned maintenance

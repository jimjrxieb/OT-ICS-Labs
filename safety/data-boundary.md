# Slot-3 Data Boundary

Allowed:

- Synthetic facility names.
- Synthetic equipment and point names.
- Synthetic point values, alarms, trends, tickets, and logs.
- Lab-only diagrams and outputs.

Not allowed:

- Real hospital or base names.
- PHI or patient-care records.
- Real credentials, hostnames, IPs, serials, screenshots, or vendor access
  details.
- Production BAS diagrams, points lists, or alarm exports.
- Active testing against real BAS or hospital networks.

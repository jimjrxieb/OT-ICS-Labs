# Sequence Of Operations -- Isolation Room 201

## Purpose

Isolation Room 201 is modeled as a negative-pressure room for BAS troubleshooting
and OT-security evidence practice.

## Normal Operation

- Exhaust command remains enabled during occupied/isolation mode.
- Room pressure target is negative relative to adjacent corridor.
- Synthetic normal range is -0.030 to -0.010 in. w.c.
- Pressure outside normal range creates a critical alarm.

## Troubleshooting Signals

- Room pressure.
- Exhaust command.
- Exhaust fan status in future simulator pass.
- Door-open status in future simulator pass.

## Safety Note

Synthetic lab only. Real isolation-room pressure requirements require qualified
facility, clinical, and code review.
